I guess it's time to write
But it's so hard to rhyme.
I open my dictionary
Many words jump up in joy,
Screaming to etch them
Commit them to the stone.

Wandering the dunes,
Infinite seeming landscapes
Begging me to escape
The desolate space of bedsheets
Remaining still
Since last you rested your legs.

No matter how far I go
How many engines I take to search
Not a single body of water
Not a single blue tinted morning
Aspires to match the deep hue
Brightening as I could say good mor...

You see, I told you,
I can't believe you were ever real
And my suspicions and doubts
Every second be clearer.
I cannot fathom the shapes of you
Belonging to a world
Where not a single sound

With your name,
A rhyme I couldn't have found.

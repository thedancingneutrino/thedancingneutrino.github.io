*\[Yami Safdie, Lasso - Otra Vida ♫ \]*
*No es tarde pa volver...*

Well, maybe it is 
And nothing breaks me more
Than building up this wall.

And although I leave this heart
In the color between your eyes
I have to walk away.

Keep it in your pocket
Or offer it to the stars
The sky as your only witness.

Tonight, I will for one last time 
At the balcony perform your ritual
And when the moon evaporates
The seawater I will be gone.

I wish to love you tomorrow
I wish to embrace you 
With no consequence 
I wish to dance a slow song
The world is vanishing,
In fact the air is gone 
The earth shook itself apart,
Water receded,
Fire burning on the corners absolute,
Only me and you
Exist in the sky.

I wish to love you tomorrow
With no consequence

But I'm in love with you today
With all the consequences 
And I'm glad.

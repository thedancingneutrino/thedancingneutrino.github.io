Not much has changed in the past 10 years.
Birds still fly out of my window
Although, now it is a different street
Singing praises I sometimes forget to perceive

The past 9 years have changed so little.
In many ways, i am still learning to speak,
And for the first time I hear phonemes
That don’t fit with my weird spanglish

Few things have changed in the past 8 years
I continue to meet new people
Who do not speak the same way as me 
But whose heart strings pull me, for a coffee or for a beer

In the last 7 years, things have changed a bit.
Still dancing and discovering
How my feet move at every beat.
As the last salsa ends, a new tango begins

‘Cause some things have changed in these 6 years.
Still learning to love, still learning to embrace, still learning to kiss.
Cause every new adventure brings a new challenge
But now I know how I wanna feel.

Truly, enough has changed in the past 5 years
Still making mistakes, still saying too much
But still surrounded by the faces,
That made me feel warmed and loved

Really, many things did change during those 4 years.
Still stumbling while walking within the same old floor
Still redecorating time and time again, the same 4 walls.
The piece will never be over, maybe this year, I will also write a song.

To be honest, plenty has changed in these last 3 years.
And change is so damn scary, I do have to admit.
Some courage and some power, and some love to fight the fear.
Cause plans are still changing, after all these years

A lot has changed in these past 2 years.
I am still rebuilding, the houses filled with tears.
But learning and growing has never been an end post.
Just part of the motion, one for which I am grateful.

Everything has changed in only 1 year.
A new spring greets me to celebrate
Cause not much has really changed
And I have changed so little
But I have accomplished so much
In the middle of these riddles.

And the room and the house,
And the dog and the cat.
My computer, my clothes, my watch.
Even my face has changed a lot thus far.

And things keep changing
And wounds will keep hurting while they’re healing
And love keeps burning as bright as 2025.

I survived 2029,
December 2032 was a breeze
2030 was a challenge
And 2034 again i would not wish

The world did not end, the world will for sure prevail.
Our dreams are still, and will be still alive. 
I welcome you all, to spring 2035.

8 pm
what an elegant time for a sunset
if you divide the day in 3,
you get 8 hour parts.
of course that would mean 
the sunset should be more elegant at 4.
In the worst part of winter, 
the sun does set at 4
4 times 4 is also 4 
whenever time is concerned after all
but 4 plus 4 is 8,
Although 8 is also 4 times 2
But 4 plus 2 is 6. 
And those are sunsets where I'm from.

8 pm is dinner for most
sitting down with your parents
with your lovers or alone.
In the old table
or on a simple chair.
The one that makes that sound when you get up,
the one your neighbors hate.
Anyway it's a dinner when the sun sets at 8pm.

Except the Spaniards 
that got stuck with a stupid time zone
But anyways, 8pm is perfect for company
sunsets with company are the best after all
in a beach in front of the ocean
sitting in a stone
Maybe with friends, with family
on a river, on a boat
on your balcony. On your safety on your own
And although I love Guayaquil's
I have to say the sunsets in Padova kinda rock.

It is an 8 pm sunset that tells me that the summer starts starting
In the middle of April through the window I'll stare.
It was at an 8pm sunset
on that same window
where you and I met
With your frame covered by gold light 
as my desire would explode
What a mysterious detail to remember
Although I imagine it's one that's hard to ignore.

Also an 8pm sunset reveals
It's all coming to an end.
The summer fades to fall and in the cold 
I'll find refuge in my friends
As august ends the days are becoming shorter
First 8, then 6 and in December
4 pm sunset.

And the sun sets differently
wherever you happen to go.
I experience as the days pass
 and I don't feel like the sun I know.
New stories are starting.
New endings are beginning.
And the 9pm shadows
Carry memories of this time
of post 8pm sunsets
as they keep fading into black.

It is curious how this season started
But I have to say 
I wouldn't change it even at my dimmest
This era once shrouded in darkness 
suddenly turned to crimson light.
And now the tales stay, 
but the lights become scarce

What a mystic thing it is for a story to start 
at an 8 pm sunset
and find its end in a Spanish sunrise.

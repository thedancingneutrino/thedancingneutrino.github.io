title: "September 22 (A New Season)"
collection: ch1

September falls slowly, the sunlight grows dimmer
And the warmth of days gives pass to winter
A certain twilight blocks the nearest sight
I watch the symbols of the clock.
But scattered in the way is the light.

Always in the cold sunrises, new stories find a home.
The mysteries of the new season, what is next to come.
This tale was never meant to be my future
Yet in my todays you are present.
What I know, I wish I knew then.

But I am tired. Allow me dear reader to say.
I keep learning and I keep earning
and I keep growing and I keep changing
I've written in my notebook, poems and stories
Danced and laughed with the beats of centuries old glories,
a thousand times.
Always turning my tears into ink and then sculpting them into art.

I'm putting my fucking foot down.

It's about damn time.
To live a story as grand
As my wildest tales can describe.
I want a mess of uncontrollable destiny
A decision is made
The opportunity is at hand

Whatever cursed war is coming in my way
I'm here. Now. Ready to fight
No longer will I be a spectator!

As September brings the equinox
So are our lives resetting.
The fog in front of me prevents
The view of what might come next.
Well whoever writes the next novel
Hear the words I'll say!

This is my fucking story!
And I'm getting my own happy fucking ending.

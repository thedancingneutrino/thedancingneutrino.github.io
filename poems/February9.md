The universe is a beautiful place

A chance encounter in the vast ... mmm noo, no..

A deep darkness in the last days of March
When we held an embrace for the last time.

The universe **IS** a beautiful place
And I'm in.... Mmmmm let's try something else

(Whistling) *Do sol si*

The universe is a beautiful place
and I'm no longer afraid to face it

If I sully with my undeserving words,
this holy shrine. The gente text of memory
I urge you to listen one last time
As I complete this diary of my grief.

One last time my face will scream
Like that fateful night in spring
As the flowers of the new year came to
I gave you one last kiss

And with the season, a brand new renewal,
Those dearest and those closest
Showed their colors painting murals.

But your shadow overbearing
kept you within my jewels.

In my dreams this pain laid so well
Like a price to visit
Something I had loved just as well

But the exhibit's season has long passed,
And from that face, only echoes of the eye.

The universe is a beautiful place,
Even looking through the eyes that cried.

A full repertoire of seasons
Has brought me poetry untold
And while feeling so dejected
I've been able to heal my soul.


A leitmotif so familiar
Carries us through one last current
Through the places you were daring
On the moments that were somber.

I remember, I remember
The tales are etched too well
Our hands first exchanged
Our bodies first connection

Warmth and comfort
and some spice lie on those beds
The ones we built together
Far apart they now remain

The universe is a beautiful place
Specially conjured with two heads

The voices arguing in yours
Shall forever be mysterious
For your own heart to unravel
Or the knife to be so vicious

I shall however admit
An orange string still pulls me dear reader
To those late and sweet Decembers
Forever they shall hold in

This memories unvisited
Now prompt me to remember
To collect and to re-gather
Relive these sweet encores

I keep what was honestly amazing
Such beauty I would never dare ignore.

The universe is such a beautiful place
I should know, I've traveled it, it has no end.

I am glad.
For it is pain that I'm discarding
I am so sad.
Cause the love we had abandons me.

It is conflicting
To grief someone who's right there.
Someone who, really, caused me so much pain.
And I'm saddened, but I still shall never forgive you.

The universe is a beautiful place,
But I'm glad mine's not shared with you.

I am crying, this is true
Cause it's a loss in the end
The loss of you
Who I loved.. so much
I am just a bit relieved
Cause I didn't have to do it alone.

But these last steps
They are mine to take.
On the other side of the door
I hear music
Oh we would have danced it so well

This house will forever hold our story
It's heart beating through the walls
Will carry the echoes of our glory.

It is, however, time to leave.
I leave your car parked here.

Now I'm getting into my old Vitara
Turn on the radio. I'm blasting a song
"Hola! señora de negro
La saludo de nuevo
Nos volvemos a encontrar"

Goodbye

The universe is a beautiful place
And To face it without you
I'm no longer afraid.
